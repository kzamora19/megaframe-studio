Shared templates

Shared templates refresh automatically when online. Previously opened frames remain available offline.

Only frame artwork and layout settings are shared. Customer photos, edits, and exports stay on this device.

Filters apply to this page of shared templates.

These frames are visible only to your approved signed-in account. They synchronize through Supabase when online; they are not stored in the public offline cache. Customer photos in your editing queue are never uploaded.

Only administrators can assign access. First create the user's email account in Supabase Authentication, then assign a role below. No passwords or administrator keys are stored here.

Administrator manages members and published templates; Editor publishes and edits templates; Viewer can browse shared frames but cannot publish. Legacy Staff retains its existing access.
Changing roles updates access on the next signed-in library refresh.
